How to use the currency converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Choose the currency you are converting from and the currency you want to receive.
  3. Use the search field to find a currency by its code or name, or tap a popular currency.
  4. Copy the result or swap currencies to calculate the reverse conversion.

About exchange rates

Rates are retrieved from public exchange-rate APIs. The converter first uses a broad public currency-rate feed to make as many supported currencies as possible available. If that source is unavailable, it falls back to a secondary provider that supports a smaller set. The date shown is the rate date supplied by the provider when available. The displayed conversion is an estimate, not a guaranteed transaction quote.

Banks, card providers, money-transfer services, and exchange counters may apply their own fees or markups. This tool is for informational purposes and should not be treated as a guaranteed transaction quote.

Frequently asked questions

Is this currency converter free?

Yes. It is free to use and does not require an account.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Are rates guaranteed to be real-time?

No. Update frequency depends on the public data provider. The tool displays the provider's timestamp when supplied.

Are my amounts sent to a server?

Your amount and currency selections are processed in your browser. The browser contacts the exchange-rate provider to retrieve rate data.

Can I use it offline?

Fresh rates require an internet connection. If previously cached rates are available, the converter may still work with those rates, which could be outdated.

Can I use this for bank transfers?

Use it for estimates only. Confirm the final rate, fees, and amount with your bank or payment provider before making a transaction.
