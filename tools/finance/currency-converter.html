How to use the currency converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Click the From or To dropdown and type to search (e.g., "Swiss" or "CHF").
  3. Select your target currency.
  4. The result updates instantly as you type.

Click the swap button (↔) to quickly reverse the conversion, or the Refresh button under the result to pull the absolute latest market data.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

We use a live public API that fetches current market data from Yahoo Finance. These are real-time mid-market rates for over 160 currencies, updated continuously throughout the trading day.

These are ideal for informational purposes, travel planning, and quick estimates. Note that banks and payment providers usually add a markup (often 2–5%) on top of these mid-market rates.

Frequently asked questions

Are the exchange rates live?

Yes. Rates are fetched from a live API that pulls current market data. The exact update time is shown under the result.

Is this currency converter free?

Yes, it is completely free and requires no account.

Does it work offline?

No. Since exchange rates change constantly, the tool requires an active internet connection to fetch the latest rates.

How many currencies are supported?

The tool supports over 160 global currencies, including major, minor, and exotic pairs.
