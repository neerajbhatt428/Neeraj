How to use the currency converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Select the currency you are converting from.
  3. Select the currency you are converting to.
  4. The result updates instantly as you type.

Click the swap button (↔) to quickly reverse the conversion.

Where do the rates come from?

We use a free public API to fetch live mid-market exchange rates. These rates are updated daily and represent the mid-point between buy and sell prices on global markets. They are ideal for informational purposes, travel planning, and quick estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Are the exchange rates live?

Yes. Rates are fetched from a public API and are typically updated daily. The exact timestamp of the last update is shown under the result.

Is this currency converter free?

Yes, it is completely free and requires no account.

Does it work offline?

No. Since exchange rates change constantly, the tool requires an active internet connection to fetch the latest rates.

Are these rates used for trading?

No. These are mid-market rates for informational purposes only. Banks and payment providers usually add a markup or fee on top of these rates.
