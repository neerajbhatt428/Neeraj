Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about privacy, file types and how the tools work.

What free tools does LocalTools offer?

LocalTools has 34 free tools: PDF tools, image tools, text tools, calculators, a JSON formatter, a JSON to CSV converter, a password generator and a QR scanner and generator. New tools are added regularly.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser, so your PDFs, images and text stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded, which is why the tools work without an account.

Which PDF tools are available?

You can edit a PDF, merge PDFs, split a PDF, compress a PDF, delete pages, rotate pages, convert PDF pages to JPG, turn JPG images into a PDF, extract text and add page numbers.

Which image tools are available?

Image tools include a compressor, resizer, cropper, converter (PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP), background remover, image to text (OCR), image to Base64, EXIF viewer and favicon generator.

How do I merge or split a PDF for free?

Open Merge PDF to combine several files in the order you choose, or open Split PDF to separate pages, page ranges or every N pages. Both run in your browser and the result downloads straight to your device.

Can I remove an image background without signing up?

Yes. The Background Remover cuts out the background with AI in your browser. There is no signup and no watermark, and your image is not sent to a server.

How do I compress a PDF or image without losing quality?

Use Compress PDF or Image Compressor, check the result and keep your original. Smaller files re-encode images at a lower size, so very high compression can reduce quality.

Which calculators are included?

Percentage, age, BMI, loan and EMI, date difference, compound interest, GST, tip and bill splitter, and calorie (TDEE) calculators. The GST calculator supports Indian GST rates.

Is there a file size limit?

LocalTools does not set an upload limit because files are never uploaded. Very large PDFs or images depend on your device's memory, so they can be slow on older phones.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open any tool in your browser and use it. If you want quick access, you can install LocalTools from your browser menu as an app.

Do the tools work offline?

Once LocalTools is installed from your browser menu, most tools keep working without internet. Tools that load a model, such as the background remover or OCR, may need to download it the first time.

Does LocalTools work on mobile phones?

Yes. The tools work in modern mobile browsers on Android and iPhone, and the pages are laid out for small screens.

Is LocalTools really free?

Yes. All tools are free, with no watermarks and no usage limits. The site is supported by ads.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use standard formulas and are good for planning and quick checks. For tax, loan or health decisions, confirm the figures with a qualified professional.
