How to remove an image background

  1. Drop an image (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF, SVG) onto the box above, paste it, or click to choose a file.
  2. Wait a few seconds. On your first use, the AI model downloads once — every image after that is much faster.
  3. The background-removed image appears right away. Hold the Compare button to see the original.
  4. Fix any mistake with the Remove wand or the Erase / Restore brushes.
  5. Keep it transparent, or pick a background colour or image.
  6. Click Download image and choose PNG, WebP or JPG.

Quality settings explained

The Refinement level controls how much processing happens after the AI runs. Higher levels give cleaner edges on hard cases (hair, fur, glass) but take longer.

  • Fast — one inference pass, light cleanup. Good for high-contrast subjects like products on a plain background.
  • Standard — adds post-processing (island cleanup, hole fill, edge smoothing). Good for most everyday photos.
  • High — adds test-time augmentation (runs the model on the mirrored image and averages the results) plus foreground colour decontamination. This is the recommended default.
  • Maximum — adds alpha matting: a trimap is built around the edge and every semi-transparent pixel gets a properly solved alpha value. This is what makes hair and fur look right.

All processing happens on your device. No image is uploaded at any quality level.

What works well

  • People and portraits — including loose hair, at Maximum quality
  • Products — bottles, shoes, electronics, packaged goods
  • Animals — fur, whiskers and soft edges
  • Objects with translucent edges — glass, netting, smoke (imperfectly, but better than a hard mask)

Complex backgrounds with similar colour to the subject still work less reliably. The manual brushes are there for those cases.

Your image never leaves your device

The AI model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your image is never uploaded to any server, no data is collected, and once the page has loaded you can disconnect from the internet and the tool still works.

Why the first run is slower

The AI segmentation model is large. It downloads once and is then cached by your browser. Every image after the first is processed in a few seconds on a typical laptop, longer on older phones or at Maximum quality.

Common questions

Is this Image BG Remover free?

Yes. It is completely free, has no daily limit and runs entirely in your browser.

Does my image get uploaded?

No. The AI model runs on your device using WebAssembly. Your image never leaves your browser.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF and SVG images are supported. Export as PNG, WebP or JPG with a transparent or custom background.

What does alpha matting do?

It solves a per-pixel transparency value for the soft edges of your subject — hair strands, fur, glass — instead of forcing each pixel to be fully opaque or fully transparent. This is what makes cutouts look natural against a new background.

Why does the first run take longer?

On first use, the AI model is downloaded and cached by your browser. Every image after that is much faster.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in Safari on iOS and Chrome on Android. Older phones may take longer because the model runs on your device's CPU.
