What is a background remover?

A background remover is a tool that isolates the main subject in a photo — a person, a product, an animal, or any object — and erases everything else, leaving the subject on a transparent background. You can then drop that cutout onto any new backdrop: a solid colour, a scene, a product listing page, a slide, a video, or nothing at all.

Traditional background removal used two approaches: manual masking in Photoshop (slow, needs skill, needs expensive software) or chroma keying against a specific colour like green screen (needs a controlled studio setup). Neither works for a photo snapped on a phone against a busy background. Modern AI segmentation models solve this by learning what a subject looks like — and they can now run entirely inside a web browser.

How to remove an image background

The whole process takes under a minute once the AI model has downloaded (which happens on your first run only).

  1. Open your image. Drop a file onto the drop zone, paste with Ctrl+V, or click Choose image to pick one from your device.
  2. Pick a model tier. Fast is the default — smallest download (~11 MB) and quickest inference. Switch to Balanced (~44 MB) or Best (~88 MB) if you want sharper edges on hair or fine detail.
  3. Click Remove background. The AI segments the subject on your device. First run downloads the model; every run after that starts instantly.
  4. Download or fix first. If you're happy, click Download PNG right below the AI panel. If the cutout missed a small patch, use the Magic wand and click the offending colour. If it cut into the subject, use the Restore brush to paint the missing pixels back.
  5. Export. Keep the checkerboard (transparent) for a PNG with an alpha channel, or pick white, ink, blurred-original or a custom colour as a backdrop.

How the AI works

Semantic segmentation, not colour keying

Older tools asked you to click a colour and removed every pixel that matched it. That breaks the moment the background has a gradient, a shadow, or more than one colour. The AI in this tool uses a neural network trained on millions of photos to recognise what is a subject — a person, a dog, a shoe — and produces a soft-edged alpha mask that hugs the outline accurately.

Where the model runs

The network is compiled to WebAssembly and executes in a worker thread inside your browser tab. There is no server-side inference step. Your image is decoded locally, resized locally, processed locally, and saved locally.

Why three models

Different jobs need different trade-offs. Fast (isnet_quint8) runs an 8-bit quantized version — smaller download, faster inference. Balanced (isnet_fp16) uses half-precision floats. Best (isnet) uses the full-precision model, the cleanest edge on curly hair, fur and transparent glass.

Tips for the cleanest cutouts

  • Start with a sharp photo. Blur or motion trails across the subject confuse any segmentation model.
  • Give the subject some contrast. A person in a black coat on a black sofa is genuinely hard.
  • Use Best for hair. If the subject has curly or wispy hair, the extra download pays for itself.
  • Fix edges with the wand, not by hand. Clicking one pixel of a solid background colour removes the whole connected region in one action.
  • Zoom in before brushing. The brush size scales with the image, so zooming in gives you a finer stroke.
  • Use the compare slider. Drag the handle to see the original on one side and the cutout on the other.
  • Check the alpha channel. If you place the cutout on a white page it may look fine, but on a dark backdrop, small halo pixels will show.

Privacy: your image never leaves your device

This is the main reason to prefer a browser-based tool over a typical online service. When you upload a photo to a web background remover, you are trusting their server with the file, their retention policy, and their security. This tool avoids all of that. The AI runs in your browser. The image is processed in memory and saved directly to your disk. Nothing is transmitted.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Background Remover free?

Yes — completely free, no daily limit, no watermark, no account required.

Does my image get uploaded?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your image never leaves your device.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF and SVG. Export as a transparent PNG or with a custom backdrop.

Why does the first run take longer?

On first use, the AI model weights are downloaded and cached by your browser. Fast is about 11 MB, Balanced about 44 MB, Best about 88 MB. Every image after that is much faster.

Which model should I choose?

Fast is the default and works well for most images. Switch to Balanced or Best if you need sharper edges on hair, fur or fine detail.

Can I remove the background from a photo of a person?

Yes — the AI is particularly good at people. It handles hair, shoulders and clothing edges better than older methods that only look for colour differences.

Does it work on product photos?

Yes. Product photos work very well — bottles, shoes, electronics, packaged goods, and any object photographed against a plain or busy background.

Can I use the output commercially?

Yes. You own the images you process and can use the output however you wish, including commercially. No attribution or licence is required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the first visit. The tool, its dependencies, and the AI model are cached by your browser. You can disconnect from the internet and keep working.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It runs in Safari on iOS and Chrome on Android. Older phones take longer because the model runs on the CPU.

What is the maximum image size?

Images up to 2800 pixels on the longest side are processed at full resolution. Larger images are automatically scaled down for editing while keeping the original for export.

Can I edit the cutout afterwards?

Yes. Use the magic wand to remove matching colours, the Erase brush to wipe small areas, the Restore brush to bring pixels back, and Undo/Redo to step through every change.
