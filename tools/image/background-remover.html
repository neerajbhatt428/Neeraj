How to remove an image background

  1. Drop an image (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF, SVG) onto the box above, paste it, or click to choose a file.
  2. Wait a few seconds. On your first use, the AI model (~40 MB) downloads once — every image after that is much faster.
  3. The original and the background-removed version appear side by side.
  4. Choose a background colour — transparent, white, black, blue, green or red.
  5. Click Download PNG to save the result.

Fix any mistake in seconds

  • Select background — click a background area (or a colour) to remove it.
  • Select object — click a missing part of the subject to bring it back.
  • Keep one object — click your subject and everything else disappears.
  • Erase / Restore brush — paint over small leftovers or lost details.

What works well

  • People and portraits — clean cutouts around hair, shoulders and clothing
  • Products — bottles, shoes, electronics, packaged goods
  • Animals — pets, wildlife, detailed fur
  • Cars and objects — cars, furniture, plants

Complex backgrounds (crowds, foliage, glass) work less reliably. The model runs on your device, so results depend on image complexity and your CPU speed.

Your image never leaves your device

The AI model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your image is never uploaded to any server, no data is collected, and once the page has loaded you can even disconnect from the internet and the tool still works.

Why the first run is slower

The AI segmentation model is around 40 MB. It downloads once and is then cached by your browser. Every image after the first is processed in a few seconds on a typical laptop, longer on older phones.

Common questions

Is this background remover free?

Yes. It is completely free, has no daily limit and runs entirely in your browser.

Does my image get uploaded?

No. The AI model runs on your device using WebAssembly. Your image never leaves your browser.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF and SVG images are supported. Export as PNG, WebP or JPG with a transparent or custom background.

Why does the first run take longer?

On first use, the AI model (about 40 MB) is downloaded and cached by your browser. Every image after that is much faster.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in Safari on iOS and Chrome on Android. Older phones may take longer because the model runs on your device's CPU.
