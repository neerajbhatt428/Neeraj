How to use the percentage calculator

Choose the question that matches what you're trying to work out, enter your numbers, and the result appears instantly. There's no Calculate button — every panel updates as you type, and your inputs are saved to your browser so they come back when you return.

What the six panels do

  • X% of Y — "What is 15% of 200?" → 30.
  • X is what % of Y — "30 is what percent of 200?" → 15%.
  • X is Y% of what — the reverse question. "30 is 15% of what number?" → 200.
  • Change A → B — percentage increase or decrease. "Price went from 80 to 100" → +25%.
  • Difference A ↔ B — how far apart two values are. "Difference between 80 and 100" → 22.22%.
  • Reverse % — you know the final value and the percent applied. "125 after a 25% increase" → 100.

The formulas

  • X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
  • X is what % of Y = (X ÷ Y) × 100
  • X is Y% of what = X ÷ (Y ÷ 100)
  • Change from A to B = ((B − A) ÷ A) × 100
  • Difference = |A − B| ÷ ((A + B) ÷ 2) × 100
  • Reverse = final ÷ (1 ± X ÷ 100)

Percentage change vs percentage difference

Percentage change measures movement from a specific starting value. Percentage difference compares two values without designating either one as the starting point. If 80 changes to 100, that's a 25% change. The difference between 80 and 100 is 22.22% because the average (90) is the reference.

Percentage points vs percent

If an interest rate goes from 5% to 7%, that's a 2 percentage point increase — but a 40% relative increase.

Common real-world uses

  • Shopping discounts — "30% off" a ₹1,500 shirt means you pay ₹1,050.
  • Tips and tax — adding 18% GST to a bill, or a 15% tip.
  • Salary hikes — "went from 60,000 to 72,000" is a 20% increase.
  • Investment returns — "portfolio dropped 12% this quarter".
  • Grade calculations — "42 out of 50" is 84%.

Common questions

How do I calculate X% of Y?

Multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Divide the increase by the original value, then multiply by 100. Going from 80 to 100 is 25%.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Going from 100 to 80 is a decrease of 20, which is −20%.

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage difference?

Change uses a starting value. Difference compares two values without designating either as the start.

How do I reverse a percentage?

If increased, divide the final by (1 + X ÷ 100). If decreased, divide by (1 − X ÷ 100).

Are my inputs saved?

Yes. Your inputs are stored in your browser's local storage and restored when you return.
