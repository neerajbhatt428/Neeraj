Split any bill — free, in your browser

This is a free bill splitter and tip calculator that runs entirely in your browser. Enter the total, pick a tip, choose how many people are sharing, and see the exact per-person amount in one step. For uneven orders, switch to item mode and assign each dish to the people who actually ate it.

Everything happens locally. No account, no friend list, no ongoing ledger. Your bill amount and any names you type never leave your device. That makes it safe for group dinners, office lunches, shared apartments and anywhere else you'd rather not paste a receipt into someone else's server.

How to split a bill evenly

  1. Enter the bill amount. Type the total on the bill, or tap one of the quick amount buttons — ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000 or ₹5,000.
  2. Add a tip if you want. Choose from 0, 5, 10, 15, 18 or 20 percent. Leave it at "No tip" if tipping is not expected where you are.
  3. Set how many people are splitting. Use the + and − buttons. Up to 100 in even mode.
  4. Read the result. The per-person amount appears instantly. Copy or share it with the group.

Even mode is best when everyone shared the same food, when the bill is small enough that itemizing is more hassle than it is worth, or when you just want a quick number to send to the group chat.

How to split a bill by item

  1. Switch to Split by item. The mode toggle is at the top of the page.
  2. Name the people. The tool starts with two people. Rename them and add more as needed.
  3. Add each dish. Enter the item name and its price.
  4. Tap the names of the people who shared it. An item with no one selected is treated as shared between everyone.
  5. Pick a tip percentage. The tip is divided in proportion to what each person ordered.
  6. Each person's total appears in the result panel. The person who ordered the most is shown at the top so you can see the highest payer immediately.

Item mode is best when the orders were uneven — one person had a steak, another had a salad, a couple shared a dessert. It's also the right choice for office lunches, group trips and any setting where an equal split would feel unfair.

You can switch between modes at any time without losing your inputs. The bill amount, tip and people count carry over to the other mode where relevant.

How the tip is distributed in item mode

When you split by item and add a tip, the tip is divided in proportion to what each person ordered. That is the fairest convention used by restaurants, split apps and accounting software.

Example: three people share a bill. Alice ordered ₹1,000, Bob ordered ₹600, and Carol ordered ₹400. The subtotal is ₹2,000. If you add a 10% tip (₹200):

  • Alice paid 50% of the subtotal, so she pays ₹100 of the tip. Her total is ₹1,100.
  • Bob paid 30% of the subtotal, so he pays ₹60 of the tip. His total is ₹660.
  • Carol paid 20% of the subtotal, so she pays ₹40 of the tip. Her total is ₹440.

This is different from splitting the tip evenly, which would charge everyone ₹66.67 of the tip regardless of what they ordered. Proportional is standard because the tip scales with the size of the order.

Rounding and why totals may differ by a cent

Every amount in the tool is divided to two decimal places. When a value does not divide evenly — for example ₹100 split between 3 people — each share is rounded individually to the nearest cent. That is why the sum of the individual shares can differ from the total by one or two cents.

This is the same rounding strategy used by UPI, PayPal, Venmo, Splitwise and every other payment system. No individual is overcharged by more than a cent, and the total stays consistent with the original bill.

If you want an exact split, choose a number of people that divides the total evenly, or add one person as the "adjustment" payer who covers the remainder.

Supported currencies

The tool supports four currencies, each with its own formatting rules:

  • ₹ Indian Rupee — uses the Indian numbering system (lakhs and crores). 1,00,000 instead of 100,000.
  • $ US Dollar — standard thousands separators. 100,000.
  • € Euro — same format as USD.
  • £ British Pound — same format as USD.

Switch currencies using the buttons under any card. All amounts update instantly.

Your privacy is protected

Every calculation happens in your browser. The bill amount, the tip, the names, the items — all of it is processed on your own device. Nothing is sent to any server, logged, or retained.

Your last inputs are saved in your browser's local storage so they come back when you refresh or revisit the page. Clicking Reset clears the saved data immediately. No account is required.

The only network requests the page makes are for the fonts and the AdSense scripts. After the page has loaded once, the tool works entirely offline. You can split a bill on a plane, in a restaurant with poor signal, or anywhere else the internet has dropped.

What this tool can't do

Being honest about limits is more useful than pretending they don't exist. This tool does not:

  • Handle tax. Some bills separate tax, service charge and gratuity. This tool treats the amount you enter as the pre-tip bill. If you want to include tax in the split, add it to the bill amount before entering it.
  • Track balances over time. This is a one-shot bill splitter. If you want a running ledger of who owes whom across multiple dinners, that is a different tool (Splitwise and similar).
  • Sync between devices. Your inputs are stored locally in one browser. Sharing the link sends the current settings but does not sync future changes.
  • Handle partial payments. If someone pays their share before you open the tool, you need to subtract it manually from the final amount.
  • Process payments. This tool calculates amounts. It does not move money. For payment, use your bank app, UPI, PayPal, Venmo or whatever your group uses.
  • Convert between currencies at live rates. The currency selector changes the symbol and formatting. It does not look up exchange rates.
  • Handle very complex splits. Percentage-based shares, weighted contributions, and other advanced splitting rules are not supported. For those, a spreadsheet is still the right tool.

Troubleshooting

  • Per-person amount looks wrong. Check that the number of people is correct and the bill amount does not have a trailing decimal from the original receipt.
  • Tip is not being added. Verify that a tip percentage is selected. The default is 10%, but "No tip" is also an option.
  • Item totals do not add up to the subtotal. Check for items with no price entered or an incorrect price. Items with a price of zero are ignored.
  • Person chip will not select. Refresh the page. If the issue persists, clear local storage and reload.
  • Currency symbol is not changing. Tap a different currency button. If nothing updates, the browser may have blocked local storage — try disabling private mode.
  • WhatsApp share does not open. Check that your browser allows popups from this site. The share opens in a new tab.
  • Saved data will not clear. Click Reset and confirm. If the confirm dialog does not appear, check that your browser allows dialogs from this page.
  • Mobile browser crashes. Close other tabs. The tool is very light but item mode with 20 items on an old phone can stress memory.
