How to use the tip calculator

Enter your bill amount. If the tax is already included, leave the tax field at 0. Choose your tip percentage, set how many people are splitting the bill, and the calculator shows what each person pays.

For group dinners where people ordered different things, switch to Itemized split. Add each dish with its price, then tap the names of the people who shared it. Tax, service charge and tip are divided proportionally — so the person who ordered a ₹200 salad doesn't pay the same tax as the person who ordered a ₹800 steak.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Both are accepted. Tipping on the pre-tax amount is technically more accurate — you're tipping on the food and service, not on the government's tax. Tipping on the post-tax is common and slightly more generous. Use the toggle to switch.

Service charge vs tip

In many Indian restaurants, a service charge of 5–10% is added to the bill automatically. This is different from GST (which is a government tax) and different from a tip (which is voluntary). If your bill already includes a service charge, you don't need to add more tip — or you can add a smaller top-up for excellent service.

How to split the bill evenly

Use the +/− buttons in Simple mode to set how many people are sharing. Each person pays an equal share of the total (subtotal + tax + service − discount + tip). Use Round total up to make payment easier — say, ₹2,500 instead of ₹2,412.

How to split the bill by item

  1. Switch to Itemized split.
  2. Add each person's name (the tool starts with two).
  3. Add each dish with its price.
  4. Tap the names of the people who shared it. An item with no one selected is treated as shared between everyone.
  5. Set tax, service charge, discount and tip at the bottom.
  6. The result shows exactly what each person owes, broken down by item.

Rounding options

Two ways to round:

  • Round total up — rounds the grand total to the nearest ₹10, ₹50, ₹100 or ₹500. The extra becomes extra tip.
  • Round each person's share up — rounds each individual's contribution. Useful when people are paying each other in cash and want to avoid paise or small change.

Common questions

How much should I tip?

In the United States, 15 to 20 percent is standard for table service. In Europe, 5 to 10 percent is common. In many Asian countries, tipping is not expected.

Should tip be calculated on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Both are accepted. Tipping on the pre-tax amount is technically more accurate, but tipping on post-tax is common and slightly more generous.

Can I split the bill by individual items?

Yes. Switch to Itemized split, add each dish with its price, then tap the names of the people who shared it. Tax, service charge and tip are divided proportionally.

What if the service charge is already included?

If the bill says "service charge" or "gratuity included", you don't need to add more tip. Enter the service charge percentage in its own field, and set the tip to 0 — or add a smaller top-up.

How does the tip get split in itemized mode?

By default, proportionally — the person who ordered more pays more tip. You can switch to "equally between everyone" if your group prefers to split the tip evenly.

Can I share the result with the group?

Yes. Click Share on WhatsApp to open WhatsApp with a formatted message, or Copy summary to copy the breakdown to your clipboard.

Is my data saved?

Your inputs are stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to any server.
