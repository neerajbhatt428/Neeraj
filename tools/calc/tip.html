How to use the tip calculator

Enter your bill amount. If the tax is already included in the bill, leave the tax field at 0. Otherwise, enter the tax percentage. Choose your tip percentage, set how many people are splitting the bill, and the calculator works out what each person pays.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Both are accepted. Tipping on the pre-tax amount is technically more accurate — you're tipping on the food and service, not on the government's tax. Tipping on the post-tax amount is common and slightly more generous. Use the toggle to switch.

How to split the bill

Use the +/− buttons to set how many people are sharing the bill. Each person pays an equal share of the total (bill + tax + tip). Use the Round up option to round the total to a convenient amount — say, ₹2,500 instead of ₹2,412.

Splitting unevenly

The calculator supports custom amounts per person. Enter the amount one person owes into their field, and the tool divides the rest evenly among the others. Use this when someone had a cheaper dish, or when one person is treating the rest.

Common questions

How much should I tip?

In the United States, 15 to 20 percent is standard for table service. In Europe, 5 to 10 percent is common. In many Asian countries, tipping is not expected.

Should tip be calculated on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Both are accepted. Tipping on the pre-tax amount is technically more accurate, but tipping on the post-tax is common and slightly more generous.

Can I split the bill unevenly?

Yes. Enter a custom amount for one or more people, and the tool divides the remaining balance evenly.

What if the service charge is already included?

Check the bill. If it says "service charge" or "gratuity included", you don't need to add more tip. Add the extra you want on top, or set tip to 0.

Does rounding up affect the tip?

Yes. When you round the total up, the extra amount effectively becomes an additional tip. This is common at restaurants — "just make it ₹2,500".

Is my data saved?

Your inputs are stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to any server.
