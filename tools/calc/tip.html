How to split a bill with friends

  1. Enter the bill amount. Type the total on the bill, or tap one of the quick amounts.
  2. Pick a tip. Choose from the presets, or leave it at zero. Tipping is optional and depends on the country and setting.
  3. Set how many people are splitting. Tap + to add people, − to remove them.
  4. Read the result. Each person's share appears instantly on the right (or below on mobile).

Split evenly or by item

The tool has two modes at the top:

  • Split evenly — everyone pays the same amount. Best for casual meals where you shared everything.
  • Split by item — add each dish and tap who had it. Best for group dinners where people ordered different things and you want each person to pay only for what they ate.

Splitting evenly

Enter the total bill. Choose whether to add a tip, and set how many people are splitting. The tool divides the bill (plus tip) equally and shows the per-person amount. Use the Split between counter to change the number of people.

This mode is perfect when:

  • You shared everything on the table, like at a family dinner or a party
  • The bill is small and everyone had roughly the same thing
  • You just want a quick number to send to the group chat

Splitting by item

  1. Switch to Split by item.
  2. Add each person's name (the tool starts with two — rename them).
  3. Add each dish with its price.
  4. Tap the names of the people who shared each item. An item with no one selected is treated as shared between everyone.
  5. Pick a tip percentage. By default the tip is divided in proportion to what each person ordered.
  6. Each person's total appears in the result panel.

This mode is perfect when:

  • Someone ordered a steak and someone else had a salad
  • You shared a few starters but had separate mains
  • A couple shared one dish between them and wants to pay together
  • You're at a party with a shared tab and want a fair breakdown

How much should I tip?

Tipping varies widely by country:

CountryStandard tip
United States15% – 20% (sometimes 25% for excellent service)
Canada15% – 20%
United Kingdom10% – 12.5% (often added as a service charge)
France, Germany, Italy5% – 10% (often already included)
India5% – 10% at restaurants, often not expected elsewhere
Japan, South KoreaNot expected and sometimes refused
Australia, New ZealandNot expected, 5% – 10% for excellent service
UAE, Middle East10% – 15%

Share the split

Two ways to send the result to your group:

  • WhatsApp — opens WhatsApp with a formatted message ready to send.
  • Copy — copies the same text to your clipboard so you can paste it into any chat app or email.

Works offline, on any device

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and once the page has loaded it works without internet. Use it in a restaurant with poor signal, on a plane, or anywhere else.

Common questions

How do I split a bill evenly between friends?

Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage, and set the number of people. The tool shows exactly what each person pays, including the tip.

Can I split a bill unevenly?

Yes. Switch to Split by item, add each dish with its price, and tap the names of the people who shared it. Each person's share is calculated from the items they ate.

Is this tool free?

Yes. It is completely free and works entirely in your browser. No signup required.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, the whole tool runs offline in your browser. You can split a bill on a plane or in a restaurant with poor signal.

Can I share the result with my group?

Yes. Tap WhatsApp to send a formatted message, or Copy to paste it anywhere.

How many people can split a bill?

Up to 100 in even mode, or up to 20 in itemized mode.

Does the tool store my inputs?

Your last inputs are saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back to them. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
