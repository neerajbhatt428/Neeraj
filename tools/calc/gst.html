GST calculator built for Indian businesses and shoppers

This is a free GST calculator that adds GST to a base price or extracts GST from an inclusive price, with automatic splitting into CGST and SGST for intra-state sales or full IGST for inter-state sales. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no signup, no upload. Just enter an amount, pick a rate, and see the complete tax breakdown instantly.

It is used by small business owners preparing invoices, accountants reconciling purchase receipts, freelancers quoting clients, and shoppers working out how much of a price is actual tax. The calculator handles all standard GST slabs — 0%, 0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% — plus any custom rate you need to enter manually.

How to use this calculator

  1. Pick the mode. Add GST if you have a base price and want the total after tax. Remove GST if you have an inclusive price and want to know the base and the tax portion.
  2. Enter the amount. Type any value — the calculator updates as you type.
  3. Choose a rate. Tap one of the common slabs (0%, 0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) or type a custom rate in the field.
  4. Select the transaction type. Intra-state splits the tax equally into CGST and SGST. Inter-state charges the full rate as IGST.
  5. Read the breakdown. The result card shows base amount, total GST, the CGST/SGST or IGST split, and the final total.
  6. Copy or share. Use the copy buttons for the summary, invoice lines or JSON. The Share link button produces a URL that pre-fills the calculator with your inputs.

What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is India's unified indirect tax that replaced a patchwork of state and central taxes — VAT, service tax, excise duty, CST and others — on 1 July 2017. It applies to the supply of most goods and services within India.

The tax is collected at each stage of the supply chain, but businesses offset the tax they paid on purchases (input tax credit) against the tax they collect on sales (output tax). The net result is that the final consumer bears the full GST, and businesses act mainly as collection agents.

GST is a destination-based tax: it accrues to the state where the goods or services are finally consumed, not where they were produced. This is why inter-state sales attract a different mechanism (IGST) than intra-state sales (CGST + SGST).

Some items attract an additional cess on top of the 28% slab — for example, large cars, tobacco products and aerated drinks. Cess is not included in the calculator because its rate varies by item; add it manually as an extra rate if needed.

CGST, SGST and IGST explained

Intra-state sales (within one state)

When the seller and buyer are in the same state, the tax is split equally between the central and state governments:

  • CGST — Central GST, half of the total rate
  • SGST — State GST, the other half

For an 18% GST sale within Maharashtra, the invoice shows CGST 9% and SGST 9%. Total tax: 18% of the base.

Inter-state sales (across states)

When the seller and buyer are in different states, a single Integrated GST (IGST) applies at the full rate. The Centre collects it and later distributes the destination state's share.

For an 18% GST sale from Maharashtra to Karnataka, the invoice shows IGST 18%. Total tax: still 18% of the base.

The total is the same

CGST 9% + SGST 9% equals IGST 18%. Whether a sale is intra-state or inter-state does not affect the tax amount a customer pays — it only changes how the invoice is presented and how the tax is distributed between governments.

Formulas used

Adding GST to a base price

Total = Base × (1 + Rate ÷ 100)

Example: Base ₹10,000, rate 18%. Total = 10,000 × 1.18 = ₹11,800. GST = ₹1,800.

Removing GST from an inclusive price

Base = Total ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)

GST = Total − Base

Example: Total ₹11,800, rate 18%. Base = 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000. GST = ₹1,800.

CGST and SGST split

CGST = GST ÷ 2    SGST = GST ÷ 2

IGST

IGST = GST (full rate, no split)

Your privacy is protected

Every calculation runs in your browser. No amount, rate or GST value is ever sent to any server. Your inputs are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device so they persist between visits — they never leave your computer or phone.

The only network requests the page makes are for Google Fonts and the AdSense script. After the page loads, the calculator works entirely offline.

What this calculator can't do

Being honest about limits is more useful than pretending they don't exist. This calculator does not:

  • Handle GST cess. Cess applies only to specific items (tobacco, large cars, aerated drinks) at rates that vary by item. Add cess manually if you need it.
  • Compute input tax credit. That requires a full GST return workflow with purchase invoices, not a single transaction calculator.
  • Handle HSN or SAC codes. The calculator works on rates, not classifications. You choose the rate based on the goods or services you are billing.
  • Generate invoices. It computes the numbers. For a full invoice document with seller details, GSTIN, HSN codes and signature, use accounting software like Tally, Zoho Books or ClearTax.
  • File GST returns. The calculator gives the tax on individual transactions. Returns require aggregating all transactions for a period.
  • Handle pre-2017 taxes. VAT, service tax and CST pre-date GST and had different rates and rules. This calculator only handles GST.

Common questions

How do I add GST to a price?

Multiply the base price by 1 plus the GST rate divided by 100. For 18% GST on ₹1,000, the total is ₹1,000 × 1.18 = ₹1,180.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive price by 1 plus the GST rate divided by 100. For an inclusive price of ₹1,180 with 18% GST, the base is ₹1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000.

When is IGST applied instead of CGST and SGST?

IGST is applied for inter-state transactions and imports. CGST and SGST apply to intra-state transactions.

What are the common GST rates in India?

The main slabs are 0%, 0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. A few items fall at 0.25% (rough diamonds) or 3% (gold, silver). Some high-value items attract cess on top of 28%.

Is the total the same for intra-state and inter-state?

Yes. CGST 9% + SGST 9% = IGST 18%. The total tax is identical; only the split between Central and State differs.

Does this calculator handle cess?

Not directly. Cess is only on specific items (tobacco, luxury cars, aerated drinks) and varies by item. If you need cess, add it as an additional rate on top of the GST slab.

Is my data saved?

Your inputs are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Is this GST calculator free?

Yes. It is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST is Central GST and SGST is State GST. For sales within one state, the total GST is split equally between the two. For sales between states, a single Integrated GST (IGST) applies at the full rate.

Which rate should I use for a restaurant bill?

Restaurants that are not in hotels with room tariffs above ₹7,500 charge 5% GST without input tax credit. Restaurants inside such hotels charge 18%. Food delivery apps usually charge 5%.

Which GST rate applies to gold and silver?

Gold, silver and jewellery attract 3% GST. Rough diamonds and precious stones attract 0.25%.

What is the GST rate on services?

Most services including IT, professional consulting, telecom and financial services fall under the 18% slab. Some services like small restaurants and economy air travel are at 5%.

Can I use this calculator for previous years?

Yes. The calculation method has not changed since GST was introduced in July 2017. Any rate change can be entered manually in the rate field.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the page has loaded once, the calculator works without an internet connection.
