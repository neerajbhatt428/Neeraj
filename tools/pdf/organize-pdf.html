    How to organize a PDF

    1. Open your PDF. Drop it onto the box above, or click to choose a file.
    2. Reorder pages. Drag any thumbnail to a new position. A blue line shows where it will land.
    3. Rotate, duplicate or delete pages. Hover a page and use the icons in the top-right corner. On mobile these icons are always visible.
    4. Add blank pages. Select a page and click Blank page to insert one right after it. With no page selected, the blank page is added at the end.
    5. Add pages from another PDF. Click Add PDF, pick a file, and its pages are appended to the end. Drag them wherever you like.
    6. Save. Click Save organized PDF. The new file downloads and the original stays untouched.

    What you can do

    Reorder pages

    Drag any page thumbnail to move it. This is how you fix an out-of-order scan, put a signed page first, or rebuild a document in the sequence you actually want. On mobile, use the ↑ ↓ buttons below each page.

    Rotate pages

    Tap the ↻ icon on a page to rotate it 90° clockwise. Tap again to keep rotating. Rotation is a page-level instruction, so the underlying content is untouched — no quality loss.

    Duplicate pages

    The ⧉ icon adds a copy of that page immediately after it. Handy when you need the same form twice, or want to keep a signed copy alongside an unsigned version.

    Delete pages

    Tap × to remove a page. The remaining pages keep their original order and content.

    Insert blank pages

    Select a page, then click Blank page to insert a new empty page right after it. Blank pages use the same size as the page you selected, so the layout stays consistent. With no page selected, the blank page is appended at the end.

    Merge pages from other PDFs

    Click Add PDF to append the pages of another PDF. Its pages are added to the end of your document, then you can drag them anywhere. Useful for combining a scanned cover, an invoice, and a set of terms into one file.

    Why organize in your browser

    Everything runs on your device. The PDFs are read, reordered and rewritten inside the page, so nothing is uploaded to any server. This makes the tool safe for contracts, bank statements, medical records, ID documents and anything else you wouldn't want sent over the internet.

    The page previews use pdf.js for rendering, and the final PDF is built with pdf-lib. Both run entirely in your browser, and the tool works offline after the first load.

    Does organizing reduce quality?

    No. The pages you keep are copied exactly as they are — fonts, images, vector graphics, hyperlinks and form fields all carry through byte-for-byte. Rotation is stored as a page-level instruction, not a re-render. There is no re-compression and no loss of resolution.

    Common uses

    • Fix scans in the wrong order — the pages came out shuffled, drag them back into sequence.
    • Strip a cover or terms page — delete pages you don't want to share.
    • Combine multiple PDFs — merge a signature page, an invoice and a schedule into one file.
    • Insert a blank back page — useful for double-sided printing and book binding.
    • Duplicate a form — repeat the same page for each person.
    • Rotate a landscape page — fix a single sideways page without touching the others.

    File size and page limits

    There is no hard limit. Practical guidance:

    • Under 100 pages — instant preview on any device
    • 100–500 pages — previews take a few seconds, especially on mobile
    • 500+ pages — works, but previews are slow on older phones. You can still add and save pages without waiting for every thumbnail

    Common questions

    Is this PDF organizer free?

    Yes. Completely free, no page limit, no watermarks, no account required.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. Every operation happens in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your device.

    Can I add blank pages between existing pages?

    Yes. Select a page, click Blank page, and a blank page is inserted immediately after it.

    Can I combine pages from multiple PDFs in one file?

    Yes. Click Add PDF to append the pages of another PDF, then drag them wherever you want.

    Does rotating a page reduce quality?

    No. Rotation is stored as a page-level instruction, so the underlying content is untouched.

    Does deleting a page affect the others?

    No. Deleted pages are simply not included in the output. The remaining pages keep their original quality and content.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. On mobile, tap the arrows below a page to move it up or down, and the rotate, duplicate and delete buttons stay visible on each page.

    Is there a page limit?

    No hard limit. Large PDFs may take a few seconds to preview on mobile.
