    How to organize a PDF

    1. Open your PDF. Drop it onto the box above, or click to choose a file.
    2. Preview. Click any thumbnail to load that page in the viewer above. Zoom in with +, out with −, or press Fit to reset.
    3. Reorder pages. Drag one thumbnail or a multi-selection to a new position. A blue line shows where it will land.
    4. Multi-select. Ctrl/Cmd-click on desktop to add pages. Shift-click for a range. Or use the Multi button above the grid, then tap pages to build a selection.
    5. Rotate, duplicate or delete. Hover a page and use the icons in the top-right corner. On mobile they are always visible.
    6. Add blank pages. Select a page, click Blank page to insert one after it. With nothing selected, the blank page is added at the end.
    7. Add pages from another PDF. Click Add PDF, pick a file, and its pages are appended. Drag them wherever you like.
    8. Save. Click Save organized PDF.

    Built-in viewer with zoom

    The top panel shows a full-size preview of the page you clicked. Use the ← and → buttons to step through pages, or the + / − buttons to zoom. The Fit button snaps the page to fit both the width and height of the viewer. When you zoom past the viewer's size, the page scrolls inside the panel — it never spills outside its box.

    Multi-select and group drag

    On desktop:

    • Click a page to select just that one and load it in the viewer.
    • Ctrl-click (or Cmd-click on Mac) to add or remove pages from the selection.
    • Shift-click to select a range from the last clicked page.

    On mobile or tablet, tap the Multi button above the grid. Then tap pages to build a selection. Tap Clear to remove all selections.

    Once you have a selection, drag any selected thumbnail — the whole group moves together to wherever you drop it, keeping their relative order.

    What you can do

    Reorder pages

    Drag any page or group of pages to reorder. Works for fixing an out-of-order scan, putting a signed page first, or rearranging a report.

    Rotate pages

    Click ↻ to rotate 90° clockwise. Rotation is a page-level instruction, so the underlying content stays untouched.

    Duplicate pages

    The ⧉ icon adds a copy of that page immediately after it. Useful for repeating a form, or keeping a signed copy alongside an unsigned one.

    Delete pages

    Click × to remove a page. Press Delete with a page selected. The remaining pages keep their original order.

    Insert blank pages

    Select a page, then click Blank page. Blank pages use the same size as the reference page.

    Merge pages from other PDFs

    Click Add PDF to append another PDF's pages. Drag them anywhere in the current order.

    Why organize in your browser

    Everything runs on your device. The PDFs are read, reordered and rewritten inside the page. Nothing is uploaded. This makes the tool safe for contracts, bank statements and ID documents.

    Page previews use pdf.js, the output is built with pdf-lib. Both run entirely in your browser, and the tool works offline after the first load.

    Does organizing reduce quality?

    No. Pages you keep are copied exactly as they are — fonts, images, links and form fields all carry through byte-for-byte. Rotation is stored as a page-level instruction. There is no re-compression.

    Common questions

    Is this PDF organizer free?

    Yes. Completely free, no page limit, no watermarks, no account required.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. Every operation happens in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your device.

    Can I select multiple pages and move them together?

    Yes. Ctrl/Cmd-click on desktop to add pages, or use the Multi button above the grid. Drag any selected page and the whole group moves to the drop position.

    Does zooming the viewer change the file?

    No. Zoom only affects the on-screen preview. The file is written only when you click Save.

    Can I add blank pages between existing pages?

    Yes. Select a page, click Blank page, and the blank page is inserted immediately after it.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. Tap Multi to build a selection on touch screens, and use the ↑ ↓ buttons under each page to reorder.
