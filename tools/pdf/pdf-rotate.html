How to rotate a PDF

Drop a PDF onto the page. Every page appears as a thumbnail. Click any page to rotate it 90° clockwise — the animation runs through 90, 180, 270, 360 without snapping back. Use the Rotate all buttons to apply the same rotation to every page at once. When it looks right, click Save rotated PDF.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Full-page viewer

Hover any thumbnail and click the magnifier icon in the top-right corner to open the page full-screen. The viewer supports:

  • Zoom — scroll wheel, pinch, or +/− buttons
  • Pan — drag with mouse or touch
  • Rotate — use the rotate buttons in the footer, or press R
  • Navigate — arrow keys or on-screen buttons to move between pages
  • Fit — one click to reset zoom to fit the screen

Changes you make in the viewer apply to the same page in the strip. Close the viewer to see the thumbnail update.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ← / → — previous or next page (viewer)
  • R — rotate the current page 90° clockwise (viewer)
  • Shift+R — rotate 90° counter-clockwise (viewer)
  • 0 — fit to screen (viewer)
  • Esc — close the viewer

Common questions

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Click any page thumbnail to rotate it 90 degrees clockwise. Or click the magnifier icon to open a full-page view and rotate from there.

Can I view a page full-screen?

Yes. Click the magnifier icon on any thumbnail to open a full-page viewer. Zoom, pan and rotate from inside.

Is the rotation saved correctly?

Yes. The rotation value is written into the PDF's page dictionary. Every PDF reader will display it correctly.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.

Does rotating affect quality?

No. Rotation is a metadata change. The content stays exactly as it was.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Tap any page to rotate it. The viewer supports pinch-to-zoom and touch-drag.
