    How to use the PDF editor

    1. Drop a PDF onto the viewer, or click Choose PDF.
    2. Pick a tool from the toolbar. Hover any tool to see its shortcut key.
    3. Edit text — click any word or number, type the replacement, press Enter.
    4. Select — click any text (original or added) to move, resize or edit it.
    5. Highlight / Rect / Arrow / Redact — drag to draw.
    6. Image / Sign — pick the tool, then click on the page to place.
    7. Right-click any element for Delete, Duplicate, Copy, Bring to front.
    8. Press ? at any time for the full keyboard shortcut list.
    9. Click Save edited PDF to download.

    Built for real work

    • Undo/redo — 50 steps of history, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z
    • Auto-save — your edits are stored in your browser every few seconds
    • Session restore — close the tab, reopen it, keep working
    • Right-click menu — duplicate, delete, copy, bring to front
    • Snap to text — text elements snap to nearby baselines when dragged
    • Quick colors — one-click yellow/red/green/blue/black

    Your work is safe

    Every change is auto-saved to your browser's local storage. If you accidentally close the tab, or your computer crashes, reopen this page and you'll be offered to restore your last session. Nothing is ever uploaded.

    Vector output — text stays sharp

    Original PDF pages are copied unchanged into the output, so the original text remains selectable and searchable. Your annotations are added as real vector content — infinitely sharp at any zoom, small file size.

    Common questions

    Can I edit existing text in the PDF?

    Yes. Click the Edit text tool, then click any word or number. The tool detects the original text, covers it, and draws your replacement in the same position and style.

    Does the editor upload my PDF?

    No. Your PDF is read, modified and re-saved entirely inside your browser.

    Will the original text stay selectable?

    Yes. Original pages are copied into the output unchanged, so selectable text is preserved.

    Does it save my work if I close the tab?

    Yes. The editor auto-saves to your browser every few seconds. Reopen the page and it offers to restore your previous session.

    Can I undo a mistake?

    Yes. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes, up to 50 steps.
