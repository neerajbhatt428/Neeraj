    How to delete pages from a PDF

    1. Open your PDF. Drop it onto the box above, or click to choose a file.
    2. Scroll through the pages. The viewer works like a normal PDF reader — every page is stacked vertically. Scroll up and down to review them.
    3. Click a page to mark it for deletion. A red overlay appears and the page number appears in the Selected list on the right.
    4. Click Delete selected pages. The tool builds a new PDF without those pages and offers it as a download.

    Nothing is uploaded. The whole process runs on your device.

    The layout

    • Left panel — file info, mode toggle, range input, and quick-select shortcuts.
    • Center — the PDF viewer. Scroll through all pages continuously, just like any PDF reader.
    • Right panel — a live list of every page you've marked, the counter, and the Delete button.

    Three ways to select pages

    1. Click pages in the viewer

    Click or tap any page directly in the viewer. It gets a red overlay and appears in the Selected list. Click again to unmark it.

    2. Type a range

    Use the input field in the left panel. Supported syntax:

    • 5 — a single page
    • 2, 4, 6 — a list
    • 5-8 — a range
    • 1, 3, 10-15, 20 — combined

    3. Use the quick select buttons

    All, None, Invert, Odd, Even, First 3, Last 3.

    Keep mode — delete everything except a few pages

    The Mode toggle has two options:

    • Delete selected — the pages you mark get removed.
    • Keep selected — the pages you mark stay, everything else goes.

    Zoom controls

    Use + and − in the viewer toolbar to zoom in and out. Fit resets to fit the viewer width. Pages always stay inside the viewer — nothing overflows horizontally. On desktop you can also use Ctrl + scroll wheel over the viewer.

    What happens to the rest of the document

    The pages you keep are copied exactly as they are. Fonts, images, vector drawings, hyperlinks, form fields and bookmarks are preserved byte-for-byte. No re-compression, no quality loss.

    Your PDF stays on your device

    The whole tool runs in your browser using pdf.js to render the viewer and pdf-lib to write the new file. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and the tool works offline once it has loaded.

    Common questions

    Is this PDF page remover free?

    Yes. Completely free, no page limit, no watermarks, no signup.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. Everything happens in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

    Can I see all pages at once?

    Yes. The viewer shows every page stacked vertically.

    How do I select pages to delete?

    Click or tap any page in the viewer to mark it red. Click again to unmark. You can also type a range like 2, 5-8.

    Can I keep only a few pages?

    Yes. Switch the Mode toggle to Keep, select the pages you want to keep, and the tool removes everything else.

    Will deleting pages reduce quality?

    No. Remaining pages are copied exactly as they are.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. The layout stacks into a single column on smaller screens. Tap pages to select them.
