How to delete pages from a PDF

  1. Open your PDF. Drop it onto the box above, or tap the box to pick a file from your device.
  2. Wait for thumbnails to appear. Every page of the PDF is rendered as a small preview so you can see exactly what you're removing.
  3. Select the pages to delete. Tap the thumbnails, or type a range in the input field — for example 1, 3, 5-8, 12.
  4. Click Delete selected pages. The tool builds a new PDF without those pages and offers it as a download.

Nothing is uploaded. The whole process runs on your device, using WebAssembly to read and rewrite the PDF in memory.

Three ways to select pages

1. Tap page thumbnails

Every page is previewed as a thumbnail. Tap or click a thumbnail to select it for deletion — it turns red. Tap again to deselect. This is the fastest method on mobile, where typing is slow.

2. Type a range

Use the input field to enter pages precisely. Supported syntax:

  • 5 — a single page
  • 2, 4, 6 — a list
  • 5-8 — a range (5, 6, 7, 8)
  • 1, 3, 10-15, 20 — combined

3. Use the shortcuts

  • All — select every page (useful when you want to keep just one or two, see the Keep mode below)
  • None — clear the selection
  • Invert — swap selected and unselected
  • Odd / Even — select every other page (useful for scanned books that have a blank back side)
  • First 3 / Last 3 — common patterns like removing a cover or an appendix

Keep mode — delete everything except a few pages

The tool has two modes at the top:

  • Delete selected — the pages you pick get removed.
  • Keep selected — the pages you pick stay; everything else goes.

Keep mode is handy when you want to extract a chapter from a book, save a single signed page from a contract, or keep only the first page of a 50-page report.

Common uses

  • Remove cover pages — strip the front page from a scanned book or report before archiving.
  • Delete blank pages — clean up scans that include empty reverse sides.
  • Redact sections — remove pages containing internal notes before sharing a document.
  • Split a PDF — delete the parts you don't need from a duplicate, and keep the part you do.
  • Trim appendices — remove the last few pages of a contract or manual that no longer apply.
  • Extract a chapter — use Keep mode to save one chapter as its own PDF.

What happens to the rest of the document

The pages you keep are copied exactly as they are. Fonts, images, vector drawings, hyperlinks, form fields and bookmarks are preserved byte-for-byte. There is no re-compression and no quality loss — the output looks identical to the original, minus the pages you removed.

One small exception: internal bookmarks and links that pointed to a deleted page are automatically dropped, because their target no longer exists. Links to remaining pages still work.

Your PDF stays on your device

The whole tool runs in your browser using pdf.js to render previews and pdf-lib to write the new file. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and the tool works offline once it has loaded. This makes it safe for confidential contracts, medical records, bank statements, legal briefs and anything else you wouldn't want sent to a server.

Works on mobile and desktop

The interface adapts to the screen. On phones, thumbnails display two per row and the Delete button sits in a sticky bar at the bottom, within reach of your thumb. On tablets and desktops, the grid expands to show more pages at once and the buttons move inline.

File size and page limits

There is no hard limit. In practice:

  • Under 100 pages — instant preview on any device
  • 100–500 pages — previews take a few seconds to render, especially on mobile
  • 500+ pages — works, but previews are slow on older phones. You can still use range input to select pages without waiting for all thumbnails

Common questions

Is this PDF page remover free?

Yes. It is completely free, has no page limit and adds no watermarks. No account is required.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device, so even confidential documents stay private.

Can I delete multiple pages at once?

Yes. Tap as many thumbnails as you want, use the range input to type something like 2, 5-8, 12, or use the shortcut buttons for odd, even, first N or last N pages.

Can I delete all pages except a few?

Yes. Switch to Keep selected mode, select the pages you want to keep, and the tool removes everything else.

Will deleting pages reduce quality?

No. The remaining pages are copied exactly as they are — no re-compression, no quality loss. Text stays selectable.

What happens to bookmarks and links?

Internal bookmarks and links on the remaining pages are preserved. Bookmarks that pointed to deleted pages are dropped automatically.

Can I delete pages from a scanned PDF?

Yes. Scanned PDFs are just images inside a PDF wrapper, so removing pages works the same way.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The tool is designed for touch first. Tap pages to select, use the sticky action bar at the bottom, and download the new PDF straight to your phone.

Is there a page limit?

No hard limit. PDFs up to a few hundred pages work well on any modern device. Very large PDFs (500+ pages) may take longer to preview on older phones.

Can I undo a deletion?

The original PDF on your device is never modified. If you change your mind, simply open it again and select different pages. You can also click Reset in the tool before downloading.
