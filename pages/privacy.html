Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Short version

All tools on this site run entirely in your browser. Your files, text, images and QR codes are never uploaded to any server. We do not require accounts, we do not sell data, and we do not track you across other websites.

What we do not collect

  • We do not upload your files, text, images, videos or QR codes.
  • We do not require accounts, email addresses or personal information.
  • We do not use advertising cookies for behavioural targeting.

What we do collect

We may collect anonymous, aggregate analytics data such as page views, referrer, general region and device type. This data cannot be used to identify you.

If we display advertising (for example, via Google AdSense), the ad provider may set cookies to show non-personalised or personalised ads based on your settings and region. You can control these settings through Google’s ad settings.

Local storage

Some tools save recent activity (such as your recent QR scans) to your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time using the tool's clear button or your browser's settings.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about the anonymous analytics, contact us via the About page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page.
