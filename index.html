Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every LocalTools tool runs inside your browser, so your PDFs, images and text never leave your device. You can check this by turning off your internet after the page loads: most tools keep working.

Do I need an account or to sign up?

No. There is no signup, no email and no password. Open a tool and use it.

Is LocalTools really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use, with no watermarks and no usage limits. The site is supported by ads.

Can I use LocalTools offline?

Yes. Install LocalTools from your browser menu (Add to Home Screen on phones, Install on desktop Chrome and Edge) and most tools keep working without internet.

Is it safe to use for confidential documents?

Because files are processed on your own device and are not sent to us, it suits contracts, ID copies and invoices. As with any software, avoid shared or public computers for sensitive files, and close the tab when you are done.

How do I merge PDF files without uploading them?

Open the Merge PDF tool, add your PDFs, put them in the order you want and download the combined file. The merging happens in your browser, not on a server.

How do I compress a PDF or an image without losing quality?

Use Compress PDF or Compress Image. They shrink files by re-encoding images at a lower size. Check the result before you send it, and keep the original if you need full quality.

Can I remove an image background for free?

Yes. The Remove Image Background tool cuts out the background with AI right in your browser, with no watermark. Download the result as a transparent PNG.

Can I edit text and sign a PDF here?

Yes. The PDF Editor lets you add text, highlights, images and a signature, and reorder or rotate pages. Scanned PDFs are pictures of text, so you add new text on top rather than editing the scan.

Can LocalTools pull text out of images and PDFs?

Yes. Image to Text (OCR) reads text from photos and screenshots in 17 languages, and Extract PDF Text copies the text from PDFs that contain real text.

Does LocalTools work on my phone?

Yes. The tools work in modern mobile browsers on Android and iPhone. Very large PDFs or images may be slow on older phones because your device does the work.

Which browsers are supported?

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. If a tool misbehaves, update your browser and try again.

Can I generate and scan QR codes?

Yes. The QR Scanner & Generator scans codes with your camera and creates codes for links, Wi-Fi and UPI payments.

Are the calculators accurate for India?

The GST Calculator supports Indian GST rates and the Loan / EMI Calculator shows the monthly payment, total interest and schedule. For tax or legal decisions, confirm figures with a professional.
